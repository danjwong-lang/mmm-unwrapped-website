Common Questions

Quick answers to help you get started

How much data do I need to get started?

You need at least 6 months of daily spend data by channel and your chosen KPI metrics (revenue, profit, LTV, or new customers).

How long does implementation take?

Most clients are up and running within 1-2 weeks from data submission. Our simple CSV upload process makes it easy to get started quickly.

What makes your pricing different?

Traditional MMM solutions can cost $100k+ annually. We've built a streamlined, automated solution that delivers the same rigorous insights at a fraction of the cost.

Can I see how the model works?

Absolutely! Transparency is our core principle. You'll have full visibility into model assumptions, validation metrics, and how insights are generated.

What industries do you work with?

Our solution works across industries - e-commerce, SaaS, retail, financial services, and more. If you're spending on marketing across multiple channels, we can help.
