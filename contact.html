How much data do I need to get started?

You need at least 12 months of daily spend data by channel and your chosen KPI metrics (revenue, profit, LTV, or new customers).

How long does implementation take?

One week from data submission to actionable insights. Our streamlined process makes it easy to get started quickly.

What makes your pricing different?

Traditional MMM solutions cost $100K-400K annually. We deliver the same rigorous insights at $20K per readout with no long-term commitment.

Can I see how the model works?

Absolutely! Transparency is our core principle. You'll have full visibility into model assumptions, validation metrics, inputs, methodology, and how insights are generated.

What industries do you work with?

Our solution works across industries—e-commerce, SaaS, retail, financial services, and more. If you're spending on marketing across multiple channels, we can help.
